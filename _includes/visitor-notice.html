Cookies

This website does not generate cookies. Future development may use a cookie to remember appearance preferences such as light or dark mode. But I have no need to know your device, your IP address, etc.

Those cookie pop-ups you see are for compliance with European Union regulations. I do not recognize the European Union's presumed authority over my website, nor would I recognize such assertions by the United States.
