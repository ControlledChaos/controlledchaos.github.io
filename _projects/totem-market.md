Totem Market is a little country store and restaurant in Three Rivers, California. I lived in Three Rivers and I knew the store owner. He first asked me to build a website for his realty agency. Then, since that went well, he asked me to build a site for "The Totem".

I initially built the WordPress site without knowing much about the store or how they operate. I have since worked there on occasion, since I was once a restaurant chef, and made modifications to the website based on inside knowledge. I also created the totem pole graphic on the site, featuring critters of Three Rivers.

The Totem website is still maintained entirely by me — security & feature updates, taking and adding photos, writing content, everything.
