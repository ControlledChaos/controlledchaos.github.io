Monica Mixes is a fun side project of a woman for whom I previously built two other websites. Those sites are still active but I will not include them in my portfolio.

One of Monica's hobbies is creating craft cocktails and she asked me for a website to showcase some of her favorite recipes. After I turned the keys over to her she has don a great job of maintaining the website. And unlike many website owners the puts thought and time into presentation.

The website operates on ClassicPress running a custom plugin and theme that I built. I created easy-to-use forms for adding recipes, ensuring that each has the same layout.

There is no logo for the project, as it is not necessary, but I created colorful cocktail images that take the standard place of a logo. A random cocktail icon is used with each page load.
